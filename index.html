The bigger picture

Investing is more than buying a stock.

The important question is not simply which company has a low share price. You need to understand the business, its financial performance, growth, valuation and the risks involved.

1

Learn the market

Understand how the Ghana Stock Exchange and listed companies work.

2

Study companies

Look at revenue, earnings, debt, margins, growth and other important financial information.

3

Understand valuation

Learn concepts such as EPS and P/E instead of buying simply because a share looks cheap.

4

Build patiently

Investing is generally a long term process. You do not need to chase every price movement.
